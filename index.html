About

About Homesick

Homesick is a Long Island emo/post-hardcore band rooted in the DIY scene. Combining melodic guitars, heavy dynamics, and honest songwriting, we aim to create music that feels as real as the shows we play.

Whether it's a basement, record store, backyard, or local venue, we're grateful to everyone who comes out, sings along, and supports independent music.
